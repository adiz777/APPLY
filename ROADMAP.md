# APPLY — Product & Engineering Roadmap

## Vision
Build a production-grade, self-hosted **Autonomous Personal Application & Recruitment Intelligence System** that empowers international candidates to discover opportunities, verify visa/sponsorship rules, generate factual tailored CVs/cover letters, and execute human-approved browser automation safely.

---

## Completed Milestones
- **M0: Environment & Security Baseline** — Repository setup, secret protection, privacy/security policy definitions.

## In-Progress Milestones (Current Sprint)
- **M1: Core Platform & Candidate Intelligence** — FastAPI + SQLAlchemy database, JWT/bcrypt authentication, Fernet secret encryption, Candidate & Immigration Profile management.
- **M2: Global Discovery, Matching & Visa Engine** — Multi-source legal job discovery, deduplication, transparent weighted matching engine, UK Graduate Visa (Oct 2027) & global sponsorship intelligence.
- **M3: Document & Cover Letter Studio** — PDF/DOCX/TXT ingestion, factual ATS tailoring, side-by-side comparison, PDF/DOCX generation.
- **M4: Command Centre & Safe Playwright Automation** — 13-stage lifecycle, version-bound human approval hashes, CAPTCHA/MFA pause safeguards, Bulk Approved Submission queue.
- **M5: Interview, Relocation & AI Project Manager** — Interactive cybersecurity mock interviews, international cost-of-living/tax comparison, live repo health telemetry.

## Future Planned Enhancements (Post-v1.0)
- Browser extension companion for one-click vacancy capture from authenticated portals.
- Calendar sync (`.ics` / Google Calendar) for automatic interview slot reminders.
- Official UK Gov Register of Licensed Sponsors automated CSV diff sync worker.
- Multi-candidate workspace isolation for university career services.
